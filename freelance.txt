Since you're a starter developer, I'd avoid the huge platforms first (like Upwork) because they can be difficult when you have 0 reviews and no portfolio.

Instead, I'd focus on sites where clients are more willing to work with newer freelancers.

1. Contra ⭐ My top recommendation
No commission fees for freelancers.
Less saturated than Upwork.
Good for React, web development, UI, and portfolio-based work.
Clients often care more about your projects than your work history.

What to offer:

React landing pages
Business websites
Bug fixes
Portfolio websites
2. Freelancer.com
Lots of competition, but easier to get first jobs than Upwork.
Many small projects ($10-$100) suitable for beginners.
Clients frequently post simple HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React work.

Strategy:

Bid only on jobs posted within the last hour.
Start with fixed-price projects.
3. PeoplePerHour
Smaller marketplace.
Less crowded than Upwork.
Popular with UK clients.
Good for website maintenance and small business sites.
4. Guru
Older platform but still active.
Less hype and competition.
Good for long-term clients who need website updates and maintenance.
5. OnlineJobs.ph ⭐ Excellent if you're in the Philippines
Many employers specifically hire Filipinos.
Lower competition than global platforms.
Opportunities for:
Front-end development
WordPress
Virtual assistance + web work
Website maintenance
6. Facebook Groups (Underrated)

Search for:

Web Developer Philippines
Startup PH Jobs
Online Filipino Freelancers
ReactJS Philippines

Many small businesses need:

Company websites
Internal tools
Landing pages
Bug fixes

You can often land your first client faster than on a freelance platform.

What I'd do if I were you

You mentioned:

You're currently working as a maintenance technician.
You're learning web development.
Budget is important.
Family time matters.

I would spend the next 30 days building 3 projects:

Personal portfolio (React)
Small inventory system
Company maintenance dashboard

Then:

Publish them on GitHub.
Host them on Vercel.
Create profiles on Contra and OnlineJobs.ph.
Apply to 5-10 small jobs per day.

Your first realistic freelance income is usually ₱2,000-₱10,000/month part-time. After you collect reviews and real projects, reaching ₱20,000-₱50,000/month becomes much more achievable.

For someone with your maintenance background, a maintenance management web app or equipment downtime tracker is actually a unique portfolio project because you understand a real industry problem that many developers do not.
